Common questions

What is Hunridge?
Hunridge is a premium apparel brand, focused on quality, designed in Dubai by PN & Co. International. It begins with men’s apparel and is built around understated design and attention to detail.
Who is PN & Co. International?
It is an independent international trading company based in the United Arab Emirates. It created Hunridge and also offers fabric sourcing and consultation to apparel businesses.
What does the fabric library include?
Three collections: Bamboo, Rayon and Fashion Blends; Nylon Performance; and Polyester and Technical Stretch. Specifications shown are illustrative, and details are confirmed on enquiry.
Do you have a minimum order quantity?
Minimum quantities depend on the fabric and are confirmed during the enquiry. Tell us your estimated quantity and we will respond with what is possible.
Can I get samples?
Yes, ask about samples when you enquire, so you can assess a fabric before committing to production.
Where are you based?
In the United Arab Emirates.

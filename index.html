PN & Co. International

International trading, based in the United Arab Emirates.

PN & Co. International is an independent trading company. It runs two businesses: a fabric sourcing and consultation service for apparel businesses, and Hunridge, a premium apparel brand designed in Dubai.

Fabric sourcing and consultation / Hunridge

Business 01

Fabric sourcing and consultation

We help apparel businesses find the right fabric. We translate a garment application, a composition preference and a target quantity into a focused sourcing enquiry, and help you choose what to sample before you commit to production.

  • Fabric selection and specification discussions
  • Sample requirements and supplier coordination
  • Commercial enquiries, quantities and lead times

For fashion brands, manufacturers, wholesalers, retailers and institutional buyers.

Business 02

Hunridge

Hunridge is our premium apparel brand, focused on selling quality. Designed in Dubai, it begins with men’s apparel and is built around understated design and attention to detail. Hunridge has its own online home for shopping.

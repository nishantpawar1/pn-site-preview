About PN & Co. International

An independent trading company based in the United Arab Emirates, behind the Hunridge brand and a fabric sourcing and consultation service.

Who we are

PN & Co. International is an independent trading company based in the United Arab Emirates. It runs two things side by side: Hunridge, a premium apparel brand for consumers, and a fabric sourcing and consultation service for apparel businesses. Running both means the company buys fabric for its own brand and understands what a buyer needs from a supplier.

How we work

The company’s approach to trade is plain. It starts with the buyer’s requirement, matches it to suitable fabrics, and is clear about what is confirmed and what is not. Specifications, availability, minimum order quantities and pricing are confirmed on enquiry, and samples are encouraged before any production commitment.

International sourcing

From the UAE, PN & Co. International works with fabric suppliers in other countries and with buyers across the region and beyond.
